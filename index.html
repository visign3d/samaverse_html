Samaverse:
A Multidimensional Artwork Universe

Project Note

Samaverse is a collection of interconnected visual universes. Each series explores a distinct generative principle, visual language, ontology, or way of representing reality. Samaversum is a speculative project exploring the idea of pseudo-life, memory, and artificial selfhood inside an imagined universe. It centers on Boltzmann Brains, unstable minds, and the strange persistence of identity within a simulated reality.
